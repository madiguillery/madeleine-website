§ 02 — About

About
Madeleine

Multi-disciplinary bio-medical artist working across the full spectrum of medical visualization — from hand-drawn anatomical study to 3D rendering and VR.

My training at the Glasgow School of Art, in partnership with the University of Glasgow, gave me a rigorous foundation in both the artistic and clinical dimensions of medical visualization — a discipline that demands equal parts scientific literacy and creative discipline.

I embrace creative challenges and unconventional concepts without compromising on scientific accuracy. Whether the brief calls for a surgical illustration, a patient-education animation, or an immersive interactive experience, I bring the same standard of care to every project.
