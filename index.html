§ 02 — About

About
Madeleine

Multi-disciplinary bio-medical artist with expertise across the full spectrum of medical visualization — from hand-drawn anatomical study to cutting-edge 3D rendering and VR.

I thrive at the intersection of art and science, driven by a mission to transform medical concepts into visuals that are not only scientifically accurate but genuinely captivating. My work spans 2D illustration, 3D modeling, anatomical animation, volumetric visualization, interactive application design, and VR development.

Having trained at the Glasgow School of Art in partnership with the University of Glasgow, I developed a rigorous foundation in both the artistic and clinical dimensions of medical visualization — a discipline that demands equal parts scientific literacy and creative discipline.

I value innovation, embrace creative challenges, and pursue unconventional concepts without compromising on scientific accuracy or artistic excellence. Whether the brief calls for a surgical illustration, a patient-education animation, or an immersive interactive experience, I bring the same standard of care to every project.

"As someone who thrives at the intersection of art and science, I'm on a mission to transform medical concepts into captivating visuals and engaging narratives focused on making health education inspiring."

§ 03 — How I Work

Process

Every project follows a structured, collaborative workflow designed to ensure scientific accuracy and creative excellence from first brief to final delivery.

I

Discovery &
Research

We begin with a thorough brief: understanding the medical subject, the intended audience, the deliverable format, and the clinical or educational goals the work must serve. I research source material, anatomy references, and existing literature before any mark is made.

II

Concept &
Sketching

Initial concepts are developed as rough sketches or low-fidelity 3D blockouts. This stage is collaborative — you review and approve the compositional approach, viewpoint, and level of detail before I commit to full rendering.

III

Development &
Refinement

With the concept locked, I move into detailed rendering and refinement. Rounds of revisions are included — I incorporate your feedback at each stage to ensure the final piece meets both your vision and the anatomical standard.

IV

Final Delivery

Completed work is delivered in all required formats — print-ready TIFF, web-optimized PNG/SVG, video files, or interactive builds depending on the project. Full source files and usage rights are transferred per agreement.
