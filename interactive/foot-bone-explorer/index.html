How to use

  • Drag to turn the foot, or use the Rotate slider. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Hover or tap a bone to see its name. You can also pick a bone from the list.
  • In Quiz, either find the named bone on the model or name the bone that is lit up.

The bones in this model

The right foot and ankle: 7 tarsals, 5 metatarsals and 14 phalanges, with the lower ends of the tibia and fibula. 28 bones in all. A bone lights up in its own colour when you select it.
