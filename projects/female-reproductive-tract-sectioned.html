The Prompt

AMInktober 2026 · Week 2

The uterus and vagina are opened to show internal structure, including the endometrial cavity, the folds of the cervical canal and the vaginal rugae. The fallopian tubes end in fimbriae that sweep toward the ovaries, and the ovarian surface is rendered with follicle-like texture. Crosshatching and line weight describe form, depth and tissue differences without color. That keeps the piece clear and reproducible for textbooks, patient education and print.
