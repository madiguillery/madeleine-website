This three-panel surgical illustration series documents pulley reconstruction and pedicled skin grafting following a multiple finger avulsion injury — from initial wound exposure and graft placement through to healed closure.

Based on a case report by Oshima J, Sasaki K, “Multiple finger avulsion with rare form of injury,” Journal of Clinical Orthopaedics and Trauma, 2023.
