About the Project

Foot Bone Explorer started as an identification app I built in Unity in 2024. This version brings it to the web, so it runs in any browser with nothing to install. Each of the 28 bones is modeled as its own named piece: the seven tarsals, five metatarsals and fourteen phalanges, with the lower ends of the tibia and fibula.

In Explore, a bone lights up in its own colour when you select it, while the rest of the foot dims. The Quiz works two ways: find the named bone on the model, or name the bone that is lit up, for the tarsals, the metatarsals, the toes or the whole foot.
