Anatomy of a Bite

The Prompt

AMInktober 2026 · Week 4

A brown recluse spider, identifiable by its six eyes and violin-shaped marking, is shown beside a necrotic bite lesion on the dorsum of the foot. Recluse venom contains sphingomyelinase D, which can destroy local tissue and produce a spreading ulcer. Healing can be slowed in patients with conditions that impair circulation or wound repair, such as diabetes. The piece contrasts the spider’s small size with the damage it can cause, and is intended to make a clinical presentation easy to recognize.
