Globe-Sparing Surgery in
Neonatal Orbital Teratoma

A four-panel surgical illustration series documenting globe-sparing excision of a congenital orbital teratoma in a neonate.

Medium Photoshop · Digital Illustration
Year 2026

Final Piece

Final plate

Process

Stage 1

Painting each stage of the procedure

I painted each stage of the surgery as its own panel first, from the initial exposure of the tumor through post-excision assessment of the globe, wound closure and the excised specimen, before composing the four into the final plate.

→ → →

The series runs from initial intraoperative exposure of the tumor, through post-excision assessment of the globe, to wound closure and the excised specimen itself. The finished plate above was composed to walk a clinical audience through the full arc of the procedure at a glance.

Based on a case report by Dr. Panagiotis Tsoutsanis and Dr. Georgios Charonis, published in BMC Ophthalmology (2021).
